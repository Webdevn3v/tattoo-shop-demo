Our Artists

Four Artists.
Four Distinct Voices.

Each artist at Inkwell maintains their own style, their own schedule, and their own waiting list. We recommend booking with the artist whose portfolio speaks to you.

01
Fine Line · Botanical
Vera Morales
Lead Artist · 10 Years

Vera's work is delicate, precise, and rooted in natural forms. Her botanical fine-line pieces have an almost scientific illustration quality — intricate without being busy.

Waitlist: 8–10 weeks
Book with Vera →
02
Neo-Traditional
Damien Cruz
Senior Artist · 12 Years

Damien brings neo-traditional imagery to life with saturated color, bold linework, and a distinctive illustrative style. His sleeves are consistently among the studio's most recognized work.

Waitlist: 6–8 weeks
Book with Damien →
03
Blackwork · Geometric
Nia Park
Resident Artist · 7 Years

Nia's blackwork is architectural. Geometric patterns, tribal-influenced forms, and stark contrast define her portfolio. Her large-scale chest and back pieces are unlike anything else in the region.

Waitlist: 4–6 weeks
Book with Nia →
04
Realism · Portrait
Elias Wood
Resident Artist · 9 Years

Elias specializes in photorealistic portraits and hyper-detailed black & grey realism. His ability to capture texture — skin, fabric, shadow — makes his work genuinely hard to distinguish from photography.

Waitlist: 10–12 weeks
Book with Elias →

Why Inkwell

A Studio Built
for the Art First.

🔐

Appointment Only — Always

No walk-ins. No rush. Every client gets focused, unhurried time with their artist. A private, calm environment that reflects the permanence of what's happening.

🧪

Medical-Grade Standards

Full autoclave sterilization, single-use needles and ink caps, hospital-grade surface disinfection. Your health is not a checkbox here — it's a foundation.

🎨

Custom Designs, Every Time

We don't pull flash off the wall. Every piece starts with a consultation. Every design is drawn specifically for you, your body, and your vision.

🔁

Free Touch-Up Guarantee

Every tattoo completed at Inkwell includes one complimentary touch-up within 90 days. We stand behind our work — because it's permanent and so is our reputation.

We started Inkwell because we were tired of studios that treated tattooing like a transaction. A tattoo is a collaboration between an artist and the person willing to wear their work forever. That deserves more care than a 30-minute appointment and a price list on the wall.

— Vera Morales, Founder

The Process

From Idea to
Permanent Art

A thoughtful, unhurried process designed so that what ends up on your skin is exactly what you envisioned — or better.

📩
1

Inquiry

Submit a booking request with reference images, placement ideas, and size. We'll match you with the right artist.

💬
2

Consultation

A free in-person or video consultation to align on design direction, placement, sizing, and timeline.

🎨
3

Custom Design

Your artist creates a design made specifically for you. You review and refine before booking day.

🖋️
4

The Session

A calm, private session in a clean studio. Music, breaks, and pacing are all on your terms.
